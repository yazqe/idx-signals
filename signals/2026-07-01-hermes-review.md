The analysis does not verify whether the oversold condition is a short‑term reversal signal or a longer‑term bearish continuation, exposing the trade to false‑positive reversals.  

- **Indicator overlap** – Both picks rely exclusively on the same RSI‑oversold signal. No secondary confirmation (e.g., volume surge, bullish candlestick pattern, or multi‑timeframe alignment) is offered, meaning the confluence claim is weak; the “oversold” label alone does not constitute independent confirmation.
