## 3. Hidden Risks  

- **Sector concentration** – All three tickers are driven solely by RSI‑oversold conditions, likely clustering in the same sector (e.g., technology or consumer discretionary). A sector‑wide reversal would simultaneously hit the entire list, inflating portfolio VaR.  

- **Liquidity risk** – No volume analysis is presented. If any of the symbols (especially WIFI, a less‑liquid ticker) have average daily turnover below 200 k shares, a 5 % target could be unattainable without market impact, turning the trade into a liquidity trap.  

- **Correlation risk** – The three positions are highly correlated (identical trigger, similar price‑range targets). The portfolio is effectively a 100 % bet on a single “oversold” signal, offering no true diversification.  

- **Chase/Timing risk** – The analysis does not check the intraday price move. If any of the stocks have already rallied >15 % today, the “+5 %” TP may be already baked in, exposing the trader to a pull‑back or gap‑down risk at the next open.  

- **Stale indicator reliance** – RSI is a momentum oscillator that can remain depressed for extended periods in a down‑trend. Relying on a single‑day oversold reading without confirming a broader mean‑reversion context (e.g., longer‑term trend, volume confirmation) risks acting on a stale signal.  

- **Indicator overlap** – All three signals are the same RSI‑oversold trigger; there is no independent confirmation (e.g., MACD divergence, volume spike, or fundamental catalyst). The apparent “confluence” is illusory, inflating confidence.
