## 3. Hidden Risks

- **Sector concentration** – Five of the six picks (CUAN, INCO, ADMR, INKP, BULL) are **base‑metal miners** (copper, nickel, gold). A single‑sector shock (e.g., a sudden drop in metal prices or a regulatory change) could wipe out > 50 % of the suggested portfolio.  

- **Liquidity risk** –  
  - *BULL* (ticker for a small‑cap gold miner) typically trades **≈ 150 k shares/day**. A 5 % target move could require **> 5 % of average daily volume**, raising market impact risk.  
  - *MEDC* (media‑related but low‑cap) shows **average daily volume ≈ 80 k shares**, which is thin relative to a potential position size implied by a 5 % TP.  

- **Correlation risk** – The metal‑miner cohort is highly correlated (beta ≈ 0.9 to each other). Holding all six simultaneously offers **little true diversification**; a single commodity swing will affect the entire basket.  

- **Timing / chase risk** – All entries are triggered by a **golden‑cross**, a lagging momentum signal. By the time the cross appears, price may have already captured most of the upside, leaving limited upside potential and higher exposure to a reversal.  

- **Stale data / back‑test leakage** – The “historical edge” figures are derived from **only 4–5 past trades**. Such a tiny sample size is vulnerable to **over‑fitting** and does not reflect regime changes (e.g., post‑COVID commodity cycles).  

- **Indicator overlap** – The analysis relies **solely on the moving‑average crossover**. No secondary confirmation (volume surge, RSI divergence, macro catalyst) is provided, meaning the signal set is **not independent** and may produce false positives.
