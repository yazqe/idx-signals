## 3. Hidden Risks  

- **Sector concentration:** At least five of the twelve picks belong to the financial services / banking segment (RAJA, GJTL, ASHA, MINA, WMUU). A sudden sector‑wide shock (e.g., regulator tightening, macro‑rate hike) could wipe out a large portion of the portfolio.  

- **Liquidity risk:** Many low‑tier symbols (CBDK, PANI, GOTO, BNBR) are thinly traded on IDX, with average daily volume often < 100 k shares. Position sizing at the suggested 5‑20 day horizon could easily move the market, leading to slippage or inability to exit at the SL.  

- **Correlation / clustering:** Several tickers are subsidiaries of the same holding company (e.g., RAJA and GJTL are both under the same conglomerate umbrella). Their price action is highly correlated, inflating apparent diversification.  

- **Timing / chase risk:** The analysis is based solely on a *single‑day* RSI oversold signal. If any of these stocks have already rallied > 15 % earlier in the session, the “oversold” reading may be a *false* signal, exposing the trader to a short‑term reversal risk.  

- **Stale data / regime shift:** The historical edge figures are aggregated over the last 5‑14 trades, but the author does not disclose the look‑back window (e.g., last 6 months vs last 2 years). If the market regime has shifted (e.g., from a bullish to a sideways environment), the edge may be overstated.  

- **Indicator overlap:** Every pick is generated from the *same* RSI‑oversold trigger. There is no independent confirmation (e.g., volume surge, MACD cross, or order‑flow imbalance). The confluence is therefore illusory, inflating confidence without true multi‑signal support.
