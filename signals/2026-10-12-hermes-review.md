## 3. Hidden Risks

- **Signal concentration**: Every pick is based on the same RSI‑oversold trigger; correlation among signals is near‑perfect, inflating apparent diversification.  
- **Sector/industry clustering**: Preliminary ticker lookup shows WIFI, WMUU, KOTA, and BREN are all in the **technology/telecom** segment, exposing the portfolio to a sector‑specific shock.  
- **Liquidity risk**: PTRO, GOTO, and BNBR sit in the bottom‑quartile of average daily volume (< 150 k shares). Executing a 5 % position could move the market significantly, eroding the expected edge.  
- **Stop‑loss arbitrariness**: Uniform -2 % SL ignores price‑action support, making the stop vulnerable to normal intraday volatility (average ATR ≈ 1.5 %). This could trigger premature exits.  
- **Timing / chase risk**: Several stocks (e.g., GOTO, BNBR) have already rallied > 10 % from their 5‑day lows; entering at the “oversold” level may be buying the dip after the bulk of the rebound.  
- **Stale edge calculations**: Historical edge figures are drawn from the last 10‑20 trades without a disclosed look‑back window. If the market regime shifted (e.g., post‑COVID volatility regime), the edge may be overstated.  
- **Indicator overlap**: RSI oversold, win‑rate, and “historical edge” are all derived from the same price‑return series, offering no independent confirmation (no volume, momentum, or macro filter).
