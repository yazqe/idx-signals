## 3. Hidden Risks  

- **Sector concentration**: Most of the tickers (WIFI, WMUU, KOTA, BREN, RAJA) are small‑cap, likely clustered in consumer‑discretionary / technology / logistics. A sector‑wide reversal would simultaneously hit a large portion of the portfolio.  

- **Liquidity risk**: Several low‑price stocks (PTRO ≈ IDR 4,160, GOTO ≈ IDR 26, BNBR ≈ IDR 66) are thinly traded on IDX. Entering a 5‑20 day position with a flat 3 % SL could cause slippage or inability to fill at the intended price.  

- **Correlation / signal overlap**: All picks rely on the same **RSI‑oversold** trigger. This creates a hidden correlation: the entire basket will perform similarly if the RSI bounce fails, amplifying portfolio risk.  

- **Timing / chase risk**: If any of these stocks have already rallied >15 % today (common for oversold bounce candidates), the remaining upside is limited and the 6 % TP may be unrealistic, exposing the trade to a quick reversal.  

- **Stale data / small sample bias**: Historical edges are derived from a very limited number of trades (10‑21). No mention of the look‑back window, regime‑shift checks, or out‑of‑sample validation. Edge estimates are likely overstated.  

- **Indicator redundancy**: The analysis only uses RSI; no confirmation from volume, price‑action, or higher‑timeframe trend. Relying on a single indicator inflates false‑positive signals.  

- **Position‑size mismatch**: All picks are presented as equally viable “Buy” ideas, yet conviction levels vary widely. Treating a low‑conviction, low‑edge ticker (BNBR) with the same weight as a high‑conviction, high‑edge ticker (WIFI) inflates portfolio risk.
