## 3. Hidden Risks  

- **Sector concentration** – All four tickers are mining‑related (coal/thermal). The portfolio is > 80 % exposed to the mining sector, creating a massive sector‑specific VaR if commodity prices reverse or regulatory changes hit mining.  

- **Liquidity risk** – PANI (price ~ 5 000) and BNBR (price ~ 82) are low‑priced, thinly‑traded stocks on IDX. A 5 % position could easily exceed 10 % of average daily volume, leading to slippage and execution risk.  

- **Correlation risk** – The four stocks move together on macro‑fuel price swings and Indonesian mining policy. Correlation > 0.8 is likely, so the list offers false diversification.  

- **Timing / chase risk** – If any of these securities have already rallied > 15 % today (common for oversold reversals), entering at the “entry zone” may be chasing a move that has already priced in, increasing the chance of a pull‑back.  

- **Stale data / indicator over‑reliance** – The entire thesis hinges on a single‑day RSI oversold reading. RSI is a momentum oscillator that can stay in extreme zones for extended periods; relying solely on it without confirming trend, volume, or order‑flow creates a stale‑signal bias.  

- **Indicator overlap** – All four ideas use the *same* RSI‑oversold trigger. There is no independent confirmation (e.g., MACD cross, volume spike, or multi‑timeframe divergence). The confluence is illusory; the signal set is not diversified.

3. **Add multi‑timeframe and independent confluence** – Require that a buy signal be confirmed on at least two timeframes (e.g., daily bullish divergence plus weekly support) and include an additional independent indicator (e.g., volume surge, MACD cross, or order‑flow imbalance) before assigning a Medium conviction. This will reduce false‑positive RSI‑only entries.
