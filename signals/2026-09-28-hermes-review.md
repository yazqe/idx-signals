## 3. Hidden Risks  

- **Sector concentration**: All nine tickers are driven solely by RSI‑oversold signals; many belong to the same high‑beta, technology‑oriented segment on IDX. This creates a hidden sector‑bias that could amplify a sector‑wide reversal.  
- **Liquidity risk**: Several symbols (e.g., GOTO, BNBR) are low‑cap stocks with average daily volume < 200 k shares. Position sizing at the suggested %‑levels could easily exceed market depth, leading to slippage.  
- **Correlation risk**: The entire list is based on the same indicator (RSI < 30). Correlated entry timing means the portfolio is effectively a single‑factor bet; a rebound in market sentiment could cause simultaneous exits and amplify drawdown.  
- **Timing / chase risk**: If any of these stocks have already rallied > 15 % today (common for oversold reversals), the entry zone may be already past the optimal point, increasing the chance of a pull‑back or gap‑down at the open.  
- **Stale signal risk**: The RSI‑oversold threshold is static; the analysis does not mention the look‑back period or whether recent regime shifts (e.g., macro‑policy changes) have altered the RSI’s predictive power.  
- **Indicator overlap**: Using only RSI ignores that other momentum or volatility‑based metrics (e.g., MACD, ATR‑based stops) could provide contradictory signals. Relying on a single oscillator inflates false‑positive confidence.
