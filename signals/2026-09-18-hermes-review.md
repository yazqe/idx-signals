Assuming they are mid‑cap / small‑cap Indonesian equities, a 2 % SL could be breached on a single‑day swing, and a 5 % TP may be unreachable without slippage. The analysis fails to assess whether the proposed trade size is realistic given market depth.  

- **Correlation**: Both picks are driven solely by RSI oversold levels, which often co‑move in a risk‑on environment. Their price action may be highly correlated, effectively **double‑counting** the same macro signal and inflating perceived diversification.  

- **Timing / chase risk**: If either stock has already rallied > 10 % on the day (common after an RSI bounce), entering at the “entry zone” (±2 % around the current price) could be **late‑entry** and expose the trader to a rapid pull‑back. No mention of intraday momentum or recent price trajectory is made.  

- **Stale data / indicator decay**: The RSI oversold signal is a **momentum‑reversal** indicator that loses predictive power in a strongly trending market. The author does not evaluate whether the broader market regime (e.g., a sustained uptrend) undermines the RSI’s reversal reliability.  

- **Indicator overlap**: Both picks rely exclusively on the **same** RSI‑oversold condition. There is **no independent confirmation** (e.g., volume surge, MACD cross, or fundamental catalyst). The analysis therefore suffers from **single‑signal confluence**, inflating confidence without true multi‑factor support.
